Quaternius low-poly animated models (CC0 1.0 Universal / public domain)
https://quaternius.com  ·  https://quaternius.itch.io

Packs used:
  LowPoly Animated Monsters   -> Dragon, Slime
  LowPoly Animated Easy Enemies -> Snake, Snake_angry
  LowPoly Animated Fish       -> Fish1, Fish3, Shark
  Animated LowPoly Dinosaurs  -> Parasaurolophus, Velociraptor
  LowPoly Animated Animals    -> Horse

Converted from the packs' .fbx to .glb with three.js FBXLoader + GLTFExporter.
Unused animation clips and all .scale tracks were stripped to cut file size.
